About

Backend engineering with a focus on performance and reliability.

I'm a backend engineer specializing in C# and the .NET ecosystem, with more than five years of experience designing, building and operating production APIs and microservices for enterprise, government SaaS and high-traffic consumer platforms.

My work centres on performance at scale. I have redesigned indexing and query strategies to bring high-load queries from ~1s to ~200ms for 20,000+ active users, introduced Redis distributed caching that reduced API latency by ~75%, and used RabbitMQ to move long-running work off the request path.

Beyond writing code, I have led a multidisciplinary engineering team, mentored developers, and taught data structures and algorithms at the University of Prince Edward Island. I value clean architecture, thorough code reviews and clear technical documentation.
